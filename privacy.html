Privacy Policy

Updated 22 August 2026.

These tools are for the author's personal use only. They access only the author's own Google account. There are no other users, no third-party data is collected, and no data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone.

Data accessed

Only what the author explicitly authorises at consent time — currently Google Tasks (auth/tasks), and other data from the author's own account where the matching scope is granted.

Use and storage

Data is used solely to sync the author's Google account with the author's own notes and files. It stays within Google's infrastructure and the author's personal devices. OAuth credentials are kept in the operating system keychain or encrypted environment variables, never in source code. No user database exists.

Sharing

None. No third parties, no advertising, no profiling, no model training.

Revoking access

At any time via myaccount.google.com/permissions.
